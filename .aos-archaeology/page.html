What to keep

Keep the house.
Refurnish the living space.

Reuse the family model, useful service boundaries and existing pieces of the app. A wholesale restart would throw away substantial domain learning. The fresh-database break needs resolving before trusting family data to an upgrade. Later, reconsider the screens and phone experience around today’s native iPhone and web capabilities instead of resuming every old plan.

Keep the memory and the craft

People, occasions, linked gifts, budgets, comments and image handling are useful material. The shared web component library and current approved design language offer ingredients for a coherent family experience.

Drop inherited obligations

Retire superseded designs, duplicate presentation patterns and blanket live-update complexity. Treat old plans as historical intentions. Track any chosen revival work explicitly in IntentTree.

The current design estate includes web tokens and a family illustration canon. The Apple token bridge still has unresolved fit and verification gaps; it is not a ready-made native app kit. A family-first product can nourish shared capture, design and collaboration practices while keeping its own household purpose.

This is a reuse recommendation. New mock-ups, product choices and implementation come after you read it.
